Margin is required. Positions are marked to market continuously, and a shortfall may be called at any time during the session without prior notice.

Orders routed outside regular hours execute against thinner books. Quoted spreads widen, partial fills are common, and a limit price is strongly advised.

Settlement occurs one business day after the trade date. Proceeds from an unsettled sale are not available for withdrawal, though they may be reinvested immediately.

Dividend reinvestment applies to whole and fractional shares alike. Fractions are aggregated across the account and liquidated on closure.

Corporate actions — splits, mergers, spin-offs — are applied on the ex-date. Open orders against the affected symbol are cancelled rather than adjusted.

Tax lots default to first-in, first-out. Changing the disposal method affects future sales only; realised lots are not restated.

Past performance says nothing about future returns. Every position carries the risk of total loss, including positions held for the long term.
